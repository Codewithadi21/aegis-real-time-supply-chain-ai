# Fictional supplier delay escalation policy

Synthetic policy example for the AEGIS portfolio; not a real company's policy.

If a confirmed shipment is expected to arrive more than 3 days late, the procurement lead must open a delay review and contact the supplier within one business day. For a delay of 7 or more days, the supply chain manager should evaluate approved backup suppliers and expedite options. An alternative supplier purchase requires procurement approval and validation of unit economics. Never assume that a suggested alternative has been approved.
